Refund Policy

When you get your money back, how it happens, and how to reach us.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

On this page

  2. Cancelling your own order
  3. Items unavailable at payment

  6. How refunds are paid

In short: whenever a paid order is cancelled, you get a full refund back to the payment method you used. You will never be left out of pocket.

1. How payment works

All orders at Fulude's Cafe are paid for online, through our secure payment provider, before they are sent to our kitchen. Once your payment goes through, preparation usually begins within minutes.

2. Cancelling your own order

If you change your mind, you can cancel your order yourself from My Orders within the first two minutes of placing it. The refund is triggered automatically, straight away.

After those two minutes, or once our kitchen has started preparing your food, you can no longer cancel it yourself. Please contact us immediately by phone so we can try to stop preparation in time.

3. Items unavailable at payment

In the rare case that an item turns out to be out of stock right at the payment step, before your order reaches our kitchen, the refund is also automatic.

4. Orders cancelled by the cafe

If we need to cancel your order for any other reason, for example because an item sold out after your order was placed, our team processes the refund manually. The same applies to a paid pre-order for a table booking that we have to cancel.

5. After collection, delivery or dining

Once your order has been collected, delivered or served, refunds are only considered where there is a genuine issue with the order itself, such as an incorrect or poor quality item. These cases are reviewed individually, so please contact us as soon as possible with your order number and the details of the issue.

6. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are always for the full amount paid for the cancelled order.
  • They go back to the same payment method you used to pay.
  • Manually processed refunds typically reflect within a few business days.
