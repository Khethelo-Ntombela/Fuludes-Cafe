1. Who we are

Fulude's Cafe ("we", "us") is a restaurant at Mapate, Thohoyandou, 0945, South Africa. This policy covers our website and online ordering service at fuludescafe.co.za (the "Service"). We are the responsible party for the personal information described here under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name (username), email address and, if you sign up with a password, that password. We never store your password itself, only a salted one-way hash.
  • Order details: the items you order, order type (collection, delivery or dine-in), preferred time, table number, special instructions, your phone number and, for delivery, your delivery address.
  • Reservations and buffet bookings: name, email, phone number, date, time, party size and notes.
  • Reviews: your name, star rating and comment. Reviews are shown publicly on our Reviews page.
  • Saved profile details: the phone number and delivery address you save or use at checkout, so we can prefill your next order.

Information we generate or receive automatically

  • Delivery location: we convert your delivery address into map coordinates to work out the distance and delivery fee. We store the coordinates, distance and fee with the order. We do not track your device's live location.
  • Payment status: whether an order was paid, refunded or cancelled, and the payment reference from Yoco. We never see or store your card number, CVV or expiry date.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used to limit repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts and to keep the Service secure. Our staff dashboard keeps a log of staff actions, which may include the staff member's IP address.
  • Sign-in session: a random token that keeps you signed in on your device (see Cookies and local storage).

Information from Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google" or "Create account with Google", we receive a limited set of information from Google. This is described in full in section 3.

3. Google user data

In short: when you use Google Sign-In we receive only your name, email address and a Google account identifier. We use them solely to create and sign you in to your Fulude's Cafe account. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service, and we do not sell or share this data for advertising.

What we access

Sign-in with Google uses Google Identity Services and the basic openid, email and profile permissions. From these we receive:

  • your name as shown on your Google account;
  • your email address;
  • your Google account ID (a unique number Google assigns to your account, so we can recognise you next time).

We do not receive your Google password, and we do not request access to any other Google data. We do not receive or keep Google access or refresh tokens; the one-time sign-in token Google gives to your browser is checked with Google and then discarded, not stored.

How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To link your orders, reservations and reviews to your account, and to show your name in the site header.
  • To email you about your account and orders (for example password or security notices) at the email address Google provided.

If an account with the same email already exists and was created with a password, we will not silently merge it: you must first sign in with your password.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Your Google name, email and account ID are stored in our secured database (hosted by Neon) alongside the rest of your account. Only authorised Fulude's Cafe staff who run the cafe's ordering system can view customer records, and only to operate the Service and support you. We do not use this data to build advertising profiles.

Sharing, transfer and Limited Use

We do not sell Google user data, and we do not transfer or share it with third parties except (a) with the service providers listed in section 5 who process it for us to run the Service (for example, the email delivery provider that sends you emails), (b) where required by law, or (c) with your consent. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. No person at Fulude's Cafe reads your Google data except as needed for security, to support you, or to comply with the law.

Fulude's Cafe's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control: revoke access and delete

  • You can stop Fulude's Cafe from using Google Sign-In at any time in your Google Account permissions.
  • You can delete your Fulude's Cafe account yourself: sign in, open My Account → Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account record, including the Google name, email and account ID we hold. You may also email us at the address in section 12 and we will delete it for you.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To take, prepare, deliver, refund and support your orders, and to show your order history.
  • To calculate delivery distance and fees, and to confirm payment.
  • To manage reservations and buffet bookings and to publish reviews you choose to submit.
  • To send you service emails: account verification, password resets, password-change confirmations, and order or booking updates.
  • To send marketing emails only if you opted in (see section 7).
  • To protect the Service against fraud, abuse and security incidents, and to meet our legal, tax and accounting duties.

Our lawful grounds are: performing our contract with you (orders and your account), your consent (marketing, Google Sign-In), our legitimate interests (security and running the cafe), and legal obligations.

6. Storage, security and retention

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 and a unique salt. All traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access to customer records in our staff system requires a staff login. Accounts lock after repeated failed sign-ins, and sign-in, sign-up and password requests are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password.

We keep information only as long as we need it:

  • Account details (including Google name, email and account ID): until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 7 days, or until you log out.
  • Verification, password-reset and password-change links: valid for a short time (1 hour for reset and password-change links, 24 hours for email verification) and then unusable.
  • Order, payment and refund records: kept for our accounting and tax obligations (in South Africa, generally five years). When you delete your account, these records are no longer linked to a login, but the name, phone number and address you gave for that order may remain in the order record until the retention period ends.
  • Reviews: stay on our Reviews page until removed. You can ask us to remove or anonymise a review at any time.
  • Security counters (rate-limit records): a few minutes to hours.

7. Marketing

We send marketing emails (offers and news) only to customers who have opted in. It is off by default. You can turn it on or off at any time under My Account → Settings → Account, or by emailing us. We do not send SMS marketing. Service emails about your account or orders are not marketing and are sent regardless.

8. Your choices and rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it (you can change your username, phone number and delivery address yourself under Settings → Details);
  • delete it (see section 3 and below);
  • stop or restrict our use of it, or withdraw a consent you gave.

Changing your password. Under Settings → Password we email a confirmation link to your account email. Your password changes only after you open that link; otherwise your existing password stays in place.

Deleting your account. Use Settings → Account → Delete my account, or email us. See section 6 for what may be kept for legal reasons. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information you may also complain to South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

9. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies or trackers. Our site stores two things in your browser's local storage so it works: your sign-in token (so you stay signed in) and your cart (so it survives moving between pages). Clearing your browser data or logging out removes them. Third parties we load content from (such as Google for fonts, Sign-In and the embedded map) may set their own cookies under their own policies.

10. Customers

The Service is directed at anyone who wants to exprience our meals.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and change the "Last updated" date above. If we change how we use Google user data or otherwise make a significant change, we will notify account holders by email or in the Service before it takes effect.
