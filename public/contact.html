Frequently Asked Questions

Payment is online only, via Yoco. At checkout you'll be redirected to Yoco's secure page to pay with your card before your order is sent to the kitchen.

Cancellations are rare. Once your payment goes through, your order is sent straight to our kitchen and preparation usually begins within minutes, so there is very little room for anything to go wrong. If you change your mind right after ordering, you can cancel it yourself from My Orders within the first two minutes of placing it, and this triggers an automatic refund straight away. After that window closes, or once our kitchen has started preparing your food, self-cancellation is no longer possible, so please contact us directly if there is a problem. If our admin or staff need to cancel your order on our end, for example if we can't fulfil it, that refund is processed manually by our team rather than automatically. In the rare case an item turns out to be unavailable right at the payment gateway, before your order reaches the kitchen, that refund is also automatic. Either way, whenever an order is cancelled after being paid for, you will receive a full refund back to your original payment method, so you will never be left out of pocket.

Our Policies

We collect only what we need to run your account and orders: your name, email, phone number, and (for delivery) your address. If you sign in with Google we receive only your name, email and Google account ID, and use them only to create and sign you in to your account. We never store card details, we do not sell your information, and email marketing is strictly opt-in (we do not send SMS marketing). You can update your details, change your password (with an emailed confirmation) or delete your account under My Account → Settings.

Read our full Privacy Policy for how we use, store and share your information.

All orders at Fulude's Cafe are paid for online before they are sent to our kitchen. If you need to cancel an order shortly after placing it, please contact us immediately by phone so we can try to stop preparation in time.

If you cancel your own order within the first two minutes of placing it, that refund is automatic and is triggered straight away. In the rare case that an item is unexpectedly out of stock right at the payment gateway, before your order even reaches our kitchen, that refund is automatic too. If our admin or staff need to cancel your order for any other reason, for example because an item has sold out after your order was already placed, that refund is processed manually by our team back to the same payment method you used, and typically reflects within a few business days.

Once your order has been collected, delivered, or served, refunds are only considered where there is a genuine issue with the order itself, such as an incorrect or poor quality item. These cases are reviewed individually, so please contact us as soon as possible with your order number and details of the issue.

For any refund related questions, reach out to us using the contact details in the Visit Us section above, and we will be happy to help.
